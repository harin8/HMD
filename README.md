# HMD

HMD is an all-in-one office management system built for Chartered Accountant (CA) firms. It gives a firm a single, organized place to keep track of everything they do for their clients — so nothing slips through the cracks and the whole team always knows where things stand.

## Who it's for

CA firms that look after many clients and need to stay on top of deadlines, paperwork, and the day-to-day work of their staff.

## What it helps you do

- **Keep client records in one place** — Maintain an up-to-date list of every client, their details, and contacts, with the ability to set aside clients who are no longer active.

- **Track income-tax return filings** — See at a glance which clients' tax returns are pending, in progress, or completed, so important due dates are never missed.

- **Manage tax-related documents and tasks** — Stay on top of tax deducted at source (TDS), certificates, official forms, and ongoing matters with the tax department.

- **Record case outcomes and rulings** — Keep a searchable record of judgments and decisions for easy reference.

- **Track your team's time** — Staff can log the hours they spend on each client and task, giving the firm a clear picture of where time goes.

- **Get useful reports** — Turn all of this information into clear summaries and reports that help the firm make better, faster decisions.

## Built around your team

Different people in the firm see and do different things based on their role — from trainees up to senior partners. Each person gets access to exactly what they need, keeping sensitive information secure while letting everyone get their work done.

## Why firms like it

A simple, friendly layout combined with practical features helps a firm stay organized, work faster, and serve clients better.
